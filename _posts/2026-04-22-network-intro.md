# Network Fundamentals Series

This repository is designed to build a strong, practical understanding of networking — whether you're starting from scratch or strengthening your foundation for cybersecurity.

It covers a wide range of topics, beginning with core concepts like IP addressing and progressing to advanced networking protocols such as OSPF. Beyond fundamentals, it also explores deeper network engineering concepts for those aiming to move in that direction.

The focus here is simple: learn by understanding and by doing. Each topic is supported with practical labs to ensure that theory is reinforced through hands-on experience.

Networking is not just about memorizing concepts — it's about understanding how systems communicate and how each component in a network operates. This repository is built to make that clear.
